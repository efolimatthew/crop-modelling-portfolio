
# Crop Modelling Portfolio

**Tagline:** Simulating crop responses to multi-stress environments using R — independent research portfolio for PhD applications.

## Overview

This repository contains a curated portfolio of computational agronomy projects demonstrating skills in crop modelling, statistical analysis, and ecological simulation. The work focuses on understanding how crops respond to interacting abiotic and biotic stresses, including drought, nitrogen limitation, and fungal disease, using process-based and data-driven approaches.

The portfolio includes:

1. **Multi-Stress Crop Growth Simulation** – Process-based modelling of biomass accumulation under varying water, nitrogen, and disease scenarios.
2. **Fungal Disease Risk Simulation** – Environmental risk modelling linking temperature and humidity to disease probability.
3. **Data-Driven Yield Analysis** – Statistical analysis of synthetic field trial data exploring drought × nitrogen interactions on crop yield.

All projects include reproducible R scripts, datasets, generated figures, and mini reports. This portfolio demonstrates readiness for computational agroecosystem research at the PhD level.
